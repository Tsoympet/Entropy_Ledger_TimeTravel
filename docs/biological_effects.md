# Biological Effects of Time Travel on Living Organisms

## Overview

Time travel, whether forward via relativistic time dilation or backward via operational emulation (heralded postselection), presents significant challenges and effects on living organisms. This document examines both theoretical implications and potential biological consequences based on current scientific understanding.

## Forward Time Travel Effects

### Relativistic Time Dilation

Forward time travel is physically realizable through relativistic effects:

#### Physiological Considerations

1. **Cellular Aging vs. External Time**
   - Proper time experienced by the traveler's cells follows their worldline
   - Minimal cellular aging occurs during high-velocity or strong-gravity trajectories
   - Upon return, biological age < chronological age relative to Earth frame

2. **Cosmic Radiation Exposure**
   - High-velocity travel increases exposure to cosmic rays
   - Blue-shifted radiation at near-light speeds
   - Potential DNA damage and increased mutation rates
   - Oxidative stress from ionizing radiation

3. **Metabolic Considerations**
   - Circadian rhythm disruption during relativistic travel
   - No intrinsic biological clock adjustment for time dilation
   - Potential desynchronization of cellular processes

### Health Implications

- **Muscular and Skeletal**: Prolonged acceleration/deceleration forces
- **Cardiovascular**: Adaptation to varying gravitational fields
- **Neurological**: Perception of time vs. actual elapsed time
- **Psychological**: Temporal displacement syndrome upon return
